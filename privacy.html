Privacy Policy

Reward Win · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reward Win ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses and protects your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Who can use the app

Reward Win is meant for users who are 18 years of age or older. If you are under 18, please do not use the app or create an account.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it).
  • Profile information: your profile photo or avatar, and an automatically generated player ID.
  • Activity and reward data: coins earned, daily reward and streak data, spin/scratch/captcha/poll/video activity, referral code and referral count, and your transaction history.
  • Withdrawal details: the payment method and payment ID (for example a UPI ID or wallet number) you enter when you request a withdrawal.
  • Task proof and support: screenshots or text you submit for app tasks and messages you send through support tickets.
  • Device and advertising data: advertising ID, device and app information, IP address and similar technical data collected by our service providers (Google AdMob and Firebase) to show ads, prevent fraud and keep the app running.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and show your coins, wallet and history.
  • To review and process task submissions and withdrawal requests.
  • To show rankings, notifications and support responses.
  • To show ads and measure how they perform.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, fake accounts, bots and misuse.
  • To improve the app and keep it secure.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following services, which may collect or process data under their own policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) – policies.google.com/privacy

Ads may be personalised based on your advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, when required by law, or to protect the app and its users from fraud or abuse. Your name, profile photo and coin ranking may be visible to other users in the ranking section.

6. Data storage and security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase. We use reasonable technical measures to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account from the Profile section of the app, or request deletion by contacting us at the email below. Some records may be kept for a limited time if needed for fraud prevention, legal or accounting reasons.

8. Your choices

  • You can edit your name and profile photo in the app.
  • You can request access to or correction of your data by contacting us.
  • You can control ad personalisation in your phone's Google settings.

9. Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page with the updated date.

11. Contact us

For questions or requests about your data, write to us at rewardwin.support@gmail.com or use the Support option inside the app.
